Should property managers roll out AI voice first? In most cases, no. The first win usually comes from property management automation that closes the missed-call gap, improves leasing follow-up automation, standardizes maintenance intake automation, and keeps property management CRM automation clean before AI voice is asked to carry more of the front desk.

If the handoff is still manual, the intake questions are incomplete, or nobody owns the CRM write-back, voice will move faster inside the same broken workflow.

Use this rollout order:
1. Missed-call text-back
2. Leasing follow-up automation
3. Maintenance intake automation
4. CRM ownership and write-back rules
5. AI voice once the path is stable

That is the practical version of an AI front desk for property managers: AI voice, SMS, and CRM workflows that recover opportunities, route the next step, and reduce manual cleanup for leasing, tenant intake, and operations teams.
