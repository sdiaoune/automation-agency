# EMC2Ops Demo Booking Targets

Date: 2026-06-10

Goal: find property management companies likely to pay for EMC2Ops' done-for-you AI front desk service and book workflow audit/demo calls.

## Offer Fit From Landing Page

EMC2Ops sells installed AI voice, SMS, and CRM workflows for property managers. The strongest demo hook is not "AI chatbot." It is a free workflow audit around one measurable leak:

- Missed leasing calls and after-hours response
- Maintenance intake, issue details, photos, urgency, and routing
- Leasing follow-up, showing reminders, no-show recovery, and stale lead reactivation
- CRM logging, tasks, notes, tags, summaries, and handoff visibility
- Owner updates and vendor dispatch workflows

## Best Buyer Personas

- Owner, founder, broker-owner, president
- Director of operations or operations manager
- Director of property management
- Leasing manager or regional property manager
- Maintenance coordinator/manager when the angle is intake, triage, or vendor dispatch

Avoid leading with generic support inboxes unless asking for a referral to the operator who owns leasing follow-up or maintenance intake.

## Qualification Filters

Prioritize companies with:

- 50-500 doors/units or enough volume to feel admin pain
- Public tenant portal and owner portal
- AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager, LeadSimple, Propertyware, TenantTurner, Knock, or CRM clues
- 24/7 maintenance line, after-hours process, or multiple office locations
- Public rentals/vacancies, owner acquisition CTA, and leasing phone number
- Support/maintenance inboxes that imply staff routing overhead

Deprioritize:

- Very small solo brokerages with no portal or visible rentals
- Enterprise REIT/multifamily operators where procurement will be slow
- Companies whose only visible business is HOA/accounting with no leasing or resident intake signal
