## Real Outreach Channel Strategy

Generic staff emails usually will not convert from cold outreach. For this niche, prioritize these routes in order:

1. **Owner/founder/operator LinkedIn**
   - Search: `{Company Name} owner`, `{Company Name} founder`, `{Company Name} president`, `{Company Name} operations`, `{Company Name} property manager`.
   - Send a short local note first, then email after they accept or view.

2. **Owner/landlord inquiry forms**
   - Many property managers care most about owner acquisition and portfolio growth. Use the owner-analysis, rental-analysis, management-services, or "manage my property" form when available.
   - Frame EMC2Ops as a way to protect owner experience, leasing response, and maintenance communication, not as a vendor pitch to the tenant-support inbox.

3. **Phone-first local opener**
   - Call the main office and ask: "Who handles operations or leasing process improvements?"
   - If routed to voicemail, leave a 20-second message and send a short email referencing the call.

4. **Referral email to public inbox**
   - Use public emails only to ask for the right person, not to pitch the whole service.
   - Example: "Could you point me to whoever owns leasing follow-up or maintenance intake workflows?"

5. **Apollo after upgrade**
   - Enrich each account for owner, president, director of operations, regional manager, leasing manager, and property manager contacts.
   - Use direct emails only after confirming the person owns operations, leasing, maintenance, or growth.

- Best initial ask is a workflow audit, not an AI demo.
